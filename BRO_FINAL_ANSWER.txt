ANSWER: NO! Absolutely NOT!

  DNS = Address (Where to find your app)
  Database = Storage (Where to save data)
  
  They are SEPARATE things!

Example:
  ┌─ yourdomain.com ────────┐
  │ (DNS points to Vercel)  │
  │         ↓               │
  │ Vercel Servers          │
  │         ↓               │
  │ Vercel Blob Storage     │ ← Your data here
  │ (NOT Hostinger)         │
  └─────────────────────────┘

Result:
  Just changing DNS does NOT change where data is stored.
  Your app will still use Vercel Blob.
