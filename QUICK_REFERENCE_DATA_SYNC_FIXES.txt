🔄 THE COMPLETE WORKFLOW
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SALON OWNER SUBMITS
   └─ POST /api/sync with type='job-payment'
   └─ Creates job: status='PAYMENT_PENDING', payment_status='pending'
   └─ Job is HIDDEN (is_visible=false)

2. ADMIN SEES PENDING
   └─ GET /api/admin/pending-jobs
   └─ Queries: WHERE status='PAYMENT_PENDING' AND payment_status='pending'
   └─ Admin sees screenshot + owner contact info

3. ADMIN APPROVES
   └─ POST /api/payments/approve with jobId (NOT paymentId)
   └─ Updates: status='LIVE', is_visible=true, payment_status='approved'
   └─ Job IMMEDIATELY visible to customers

4. CUSTOMERS SEE JOB
   └─ GET /api/sync with type='live-jobs'
   └─ Queries: WHERE status='LIVE' AND is_visible=true
   └─ Customers can apply
